Score definition

Aleph formula

Aleph(t) = ∑i=16 wi · Ci(t)

Each component Ci is scored from 0–100. Weights sum to 1.00. If any component is unavailable, Aleph is unavailable; the remaining weights are not rescaled.

What the components mean
  • Energy adequacy: equal blend of total-load and critical-load service.
  • Physical efficiency: served energy divided by external energy, adjusted for net stored-energy change. Battery discharge is not counted as a new source.
  • Renewable utilization: PV used divided by PV generated.
  • Storage: charge/discharge efficiency adjusted by the optimizer’s linear degradation-cost proxy.
  • Economics: candidate tariff cost compared with the measured baseline bill under the configured synthetic schedule.
  • Network checks: persisted LP checks only; this is not an AC voltage or feeder thermal-feasibility proof.

Tariff rates and one-to-one export credits are synthetic demo assumptions, not a utility tariff or export agreement. The optimizer has no export decision variable.
